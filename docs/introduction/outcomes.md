# Expected Outcomes

## Objectives

| Objective                             | Target                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enable minimal-personnel operations   | Full data center operation with a small team - in normal conditions and during adverse events        |
| Reduce Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) | 80–95% reduction through automated identification and remediation of known issues                    |
| Reduce manual operational hours       | 80–90% reduction through automated routine tasks, root cause analysis, and recommendation generation |
| Support air-gapped environments       | Full operation with zero external network dependencies                                               |
| EU AI Act compliance                  | Classified as a high-risk AI system under Annex III; compliance obligations are specified in full    |

## Target scope

Autonomous Operations targets data centers ranging from small deployments (a few racks) to large enterprise and colocation facilities (a few thousand racks per region). Hyperscale data centers (hundreds of thousands of racks per region) are explicitly out of scope.

The architecture enables teams to quickly replicate and adapt deployments across different data center setups, including air-gapped and multi-vendor environments.

## Project context

Autonomous Operations is developed by SAP as part of the [IPCEI-CIS](https://ipcei-cis.eu) initiative to strengthen European digital sovereignty by providing provider-independent cloud-to-edge infrastructure components. Within the broader [Apeiro reference architecture](https://apeirora.eu/), AO is the subsystem responsible for automated data center operations across heterogeneous, multi-vendor infrastructure spanning servers, network devices, storage systems, and power infrastructure.
